Scope: replace only the second AI-Pilot “How it works” card's image with a looping movie. The complete page was read first: its four stages distinguish perception, route adaptation, multi-system teaming, and resilience without GPS/comms. This figure explains route adaptation, not the other stages.

## Object and truth model

A miniature flight-test terrain on a machined bench. A drone travels toward a waypoint; a retractable obstacle rises into its original corridor. It hovers while a new route is revealed, follows that route around the obstruction, and returns after the obstruction retracts. The physical obstacle is the reason the path changes.

This is a conceptual demonstration, not flight-control software or measured aircraft performance. Distances and time are illustrative. One deterministic model drives the route, obstacle height, aircraft position and preview readout. The route must maintain clearance against the actual obstacle and static terrain. The 14-second loop returns to its starting pose without a fade or jump.
